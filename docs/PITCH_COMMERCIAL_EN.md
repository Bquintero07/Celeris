# Celeris — Commercial Pitch Script

**Format:** Presentation + live demo  
**Target audience:** Event agency owners, operations directors, agency group CTOs  
**Duration:** ≤ 8 minutes  
**Tone:** Confident, direct — show, don't tell

---

## [1:00 – 2:30] The Problem

**Three compounding problems:**

**1. Fragmented operations**  
A typical event has 6 to 12 people involved — comercial, logistics, production, finance, suppliers. Every handoff is a WhatsApp message or a shared Google Sheet that someone is editing at the same time as someone else.

**2. Invisible margins**  
Most event companies don't know their real margin until the event is over and all the supplier invoices arrive. By then, it's too late to fix anything.

**3. Zero approval controls**  
Quotes go to clients before the director has seen them. Costs get approved verbally. There's no paper trail. When a dispute happens, the company can't prove anything.

> "The result: event companies systematically undercharge, overpay suppliers, and have no data to negotiate better rates next year."

---

## [2:30 – 4:30] The Solution — Celeris

> "Celeris is the operating system for event operations."

**One workspace. Three capabilities:**

### Capability 1: AI-powered quote generation (30 seconds)
Type a description of your event. Celeris generates a full Bill of Materials — personnel, equipment, catering, logistics — based on your historical costs and available inventory. Your team reviews and edits, then applies it. One click.

### Capability 2: Real-time margin visibility (always on)
Every item has a selling price and a base cost. Celeris calculates your margin, your markup percentage, and your profitability in real time — and only shows it to the people who should see it. Logistics sees crew assignments. Finance sees the full picture.

### Capability 3: Approval flow with audit trail
A quote starts as a draft. The comercial submits it for review. The director approves it. Only then does a PDF go to the client — under your brand, with your colors, with IVA calculated correctly. Every step is logged with timestamps and actors.

## [4:30 – 5:30] White-label and Multi-tenant

> "Here's what makes Celeris different from a generic project management tool."

**It's fully white-labeled.** Your clients never see "Celeris." They see your agency's name, your logo, your brand colors — on every invoice, every email, every screen.

**It's multi-tenant by design.** An agency group can run 10 sub-brands on the same platform. Each one has its own data, its own team, its own module set. A boutique agency doesn't pay for features it doesn't need.

**It's modular.** You can enable or disable: Events & Quotes, Inventory, Crew management, Analytics, Billing, and the AI assistant — per tenant.
